About

I'm a Junior Research Fellow at IIT Delhi, working on an ICMR-funded project in collaboration with AIIMS Delhi. My work focuses on CT-based radiomics for early-stage lung metastasis classification in sarcoma patients — from raw DICOM data to interpretable, clinically-grounded ML pipelines.

I build end-to-end systems: feature extraction with PyRadiomics, ICC-based filtering, nested cross-validation, ensemble classifiers, calibration, and SHAP-based explainability. I care about pipelines that hold up under scrutiny — both statistical and clinical.

Outside the lab, I've shipped a deployed heart disease risk app and written on prediabetes prediction. I'm finishing my B.Tech in CSE at Bennett University and actively looking for DS/ML and Data Engineering roles in Delhi NCR.
