About

I'm a Machine Learning researcher with a strong focus on healthcare AI, currently contributing to an ICMR-funded research project at IIT Delhi in collaboration with AIIMS Delhi. My work centers around lung nodule detection and sarcoma metastasis classification using radiomics, advanced ML models, and explainability techniques like SHAP.

With hands-on experience in building end-to-end ML pipelines, I specialize in data preprocessing, feature engineering, SMOTE for imbalance handling, cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning, and model optimization. I work extensively with classification models including Random Forest, Decision Trees, and other ensemble methods, ensuring both performance and interpretability in high-stakes medical applications.

Beyond research, I have authored research papers, built healthcare-focused applications like CardioCare (Heart Health Prediction App), and actively contributed to leadership roles at university. My interests lie in AI research, data science, and building scalable ML systems for real-world impact.
